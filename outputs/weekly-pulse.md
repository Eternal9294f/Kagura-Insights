# Weekly Product Pulse — Groww app

**Week of 14 Oct 2026** · 60 reviews · 22 Jul 2026 – 14 Oct 2026

**Top themes**
1. **App stability & performance** — 20 reviews (33%): crashes, hangs and lag, worst around market open.
2. **Pricing & charges** — 15 reviews (25%): redemption deductions read as hidden fees, led by exit load (12 mentions, 10 confused).
3. **Support & KYC** — 10 reviews (17%): tickets open 5–10 days, template replies, no callbacks.

**Quotes**
- “Loading spinner forever on the order page have to restart the app”
- “Complaint about incorrect charges still open after two weeks zero resolution”
- “KYC re verification demanded out of nowhere and my SIP was paused”

**Key observation**
Stability is the loudest theme by volume, but the sharpest pain is exit load confusion: 10 of 12 mentions treat the deduction as a Groww charge, not a fund-house cost. Crashes cluster 09:15–09:30 IST; charge complaints cluster within days of first redemption.

**Action ideas**
1. Show estimated exit load (amount + %) on the redeem confirmation screen, linking the scheme's exit-load terms.
2. Market-hours crash budget: alert on any crash 09:15–09:30 IST; freeze releases during that window.
3. In-app fee explainer card on fund pages (exit load, expense ratio) + attach redemption transaction IDs to charge-related tickets.
